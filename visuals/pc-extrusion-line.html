    Inside the machine

    Two cutaways of the parts that decide sheet quality: the vented screw that melts and degasses the resin, and the die and calender that set width, thickness and surface.

    All figures are typical for a 2.1 m PC solid-sheet line and are given for orientation, not as setpoints. Confirm drying and melt temperatures with the resin datasheet and the line maker's manual. Output assumes 1.20 g/cm³ density, 2.05 m net width plus edge trim, and a line capped at about 1,400 kg/h.

    Downstream handling (masking removal, pre-drying finished sheet at 120 °C for about 2 h per mm, not exceeding 125 °C) follows the SABIC LEXAN Sheet Processing Guide (SFS-6212, Nov 2015) in this repository.
